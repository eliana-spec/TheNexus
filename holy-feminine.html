Inside the mini-course

Ten lessons. Ten minutes each. One life-changing vision.

Each lesson opens one piece of the Torah's vision for women, relationships and the world at the threshold of Redemption.

  1. 01
    Lesson 1 · 10 minStart here

    Tranquil Daughters, Rise!

    Introducing a roadmap for women who want to live deeply, connect truly and rise into their fullest potential. Torah's inner light, our feminine awakening, and the wisdom for our time.

  2. 02
    Lesson 2 · 10 min

    The Question that Opens the Door

    Breaking the invisible ceiling: how diving into deep sources reveals a new dimension of feminine spiritual growth, within everything we already received in our mesorah. Explore why women are central to Redemption, and how this clarity can transform shame into light.

  3. 03
    Lesson 3 · 10 min

    The Pre-Messianic Woman

    Redemption is seeping in… are you feeling it? We're in the "Erev Shabbos" of world history: messy, but unstoppable. The Torah maps the rise of holy femininity and the secrets of Redemption carried within the Pre-Messianic Woman.

  4. 04
    Lesson 4 · 10 min

    The Moon & the Feminine Odyssey

    Understand why the moon was made small, her protests and appeasement, and how the feminine rising is a recovery of original brilliance.

  5. 05
    Lesson 5 · 10 min

    The Lost Princess & the Shechinah

    A story of longing, persistence, and an unfinished ending that depends on us. Understand how the Princess represents the Shechinah, the trials of the Viceroy, and why mitzvos are the currency of Redemption.

  6. 06
    Lesson 6 · 10 min

    Adam, Chava and the Triple Beat

    How the story of Adam and Chava mirrors the sun and moon, why every descent leads to a greater ascent, and how masculine and feminine, face to face, become the very crown of Redemption.

  7. 07
    Lesson 7 · 10 min

    Demystifying the Mystical: An Introduction to P'nimiyus HaTorah

    Unbelievable depth that is so, so beautiful. Discover what the Sefiros are, why they're holographic (and what that even means!), and how these teachings aren't just ideas… they transform our lives, marriages and avodah.

  8. 08
    Lesson 8 · 10 min

    The Seven Stages of Feminine Development

    Follow the Arizal's remarkable map of the feminine journey: shrinking to a point, rising through instinct, emotion, intellect and crown. A roadmap showing exactly how the feminine regains her light, and how to be in transition between levels.

  9. 09
    Lesson 9 · 10 min

    Marriage, True Love and a Cosmic Story

    As women rise, marriage can rise too. What will this look like, in kedushah? How can we lift marriage into a union that mirrors Hashem and the Shechinah? The feminine gift of receiving, the new gift of giving, and the beautiful secret of "sharing a crown."

  10. 10
    Lesson 10 · 10 min

    The Women in Torah Guide Us to Redemption

    From Sarah to Esther, the Imahos show us how the feminine rises, carries the merit of Redemption, and lights the path for our generation.

Is it really free?

Yes, 100% free. Enroll with your name and email and all ten lessons open for you. No credit card, no trial, no catch.

Is this rooted in our mesorah?

Yes. Every lesson draws on classical, Chassidic and kabbalistic sources, and the Nexus School is guided and endorsed by leading Rabbanim, including HaRav Aharon Lopiansky and HaRav Yitzchak Berkovitz.

How much time does it take?

Each lesson is about 10 minutes, so the whole mini-course takes under two hours. Many women do one lesson a day; some finish all ten in a single evening. Go at whatever pace suits you.

Do I need a background in Chassidus or Kabbalah?

Not at all. The mini-course is designed as an entry point. Tamar explains every concept clearly, and Lesson 7 is a gentle introduction to P'nimiyus HaTorah for anyone meeting these ideas for the first time.

Who is this mini-course for?

Jewish women who love Torah and are hungry for more depth: more meaning in their avodah, their marriages and their sense of who Hashem created them to be.

How do I watch or listen?

Once you join, your lessons are delivered to your inbox. Watch or listen wherever suits you: Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, TorahAnytime or the Nexus School site, on your phone, tablet or computer.
