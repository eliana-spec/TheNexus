The Rise! Method

A year that changes you from the inside out.

For twelve months, you learn with Rebbetzin Tamar Taback, moving slowly and deliberately through the lives of Chavah, Sarah, Rivkah, Rachel and Leah. One Imma at a time.

Drawing on classical, Chassidic and mystical sources, Rise! makes some of the deepest teachings of our mesorah accessible — and brings them into direct conversation with a woman's identity, relationships, marriage, motherhood, prayer, healing and purpose.

But the Torah doesn't simply arrive in your inbox.

Each week includes guided reflection and practical integration: the self-awareness and inner work that allow what you learn to move from the mind into the way you understand yourself and live your life.

Rise! does not begin with an outside framework and then try to fit Torah around it. It begins with Torah itself — with the conviction that the deepest questions of our generation are answered by the deepest teachings of our tradition.

Week by week, the Torah begins to name what you couldn't name. It gives language to parts of yourself that never quite fit. It reveals that they may never have been the problem.

This is intellectual learning that becomes personal transformation. Not by adding another layer of who you think you should be — but by uncovering who you have always been.

Who Rise! Is For

For the woman who doesn't want to stop at inspiration.

You may have a rich background in Torah learning. You may have almost none. What matters is the thirst.

Rise! is for the woman who wants Torah that is sound, deep and authentic — and also profoundly relevant to her inner life. The woman who is ready to question, learn, reflect and discover herself within Torah, rather than somewhere outside it.

You don't need to be a scholar. You don't need to have done anything like this before. You need to be willing to show up — and let a year of Torah do its work.
